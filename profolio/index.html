Page 02 · About

Get to know me

Profile Photo

I'm Imran Khan, a full-stack developer and product designer based in New York. Over the past five years I've helped startups and established companies design, build, and ship digital products that people actually enjoy using.

My approach blends solid engineering with a strong eye for design — I care as much about how something looks and feels as how it performs under the hood. I've worked across fintech, health, and logistics, usually joining early to help shape both the product and the codebase.

When I'm not building things for clients, I'm usually experimenting with side projects, reading about design systems, or mentoring newer developers.
